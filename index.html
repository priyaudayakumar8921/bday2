HER STORY

A Journey Through Time

On 28 October 2004, the beloved daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Krishnakumar arrived and began a story destined to fill hearts with love, laughter, and happiness.

  1. 2004 · Chapter 01

    The Beginning

    A tiny bundle of joy, a brand-new story, and a family whose world grew brighter. Her journey began in the loving arms of the two people who would always be her first home.

  2. 2005–2009 · Chapter 02

    Little Moments, Big Memories

    Innocent smiles, playful adventures, and an endless curiosity made the little things unforgettable. Every photograph held a piece of a childhood that would never come again.

  3. 2010–2015 · Chapter 03

    Growing Up

    New friends, school days, laughter, and lessons opened up a world of new experiences. Little by little, she discovered the world around her and the person she was becoming.

  4. 2016–2020 · Chapter 04

    Becoming Her Own Person

    Dreams changed and grew, confidence blossomed, and friendships became unforgettable. As childhood slowly gave way to a new chapter, a world of possibilities opened up.

  5. 2020–2022 · Chapter 05

    The School Days

    Lessons reached far beyond textbooks, and friendships became memories to keep. When the final school bell rang, one chapter closed gently to make room for a new beginning.

  6. 2022–2026 · Chapter 06

    The College Chronicles

    New beginnings, bigger dreams, and unexpected experiences shaped her in new ways. College became more than an academic journey: it was a place to grow, discover herself, and make memories for a lifetime.

  7. 2026 · Chapter 07

    The Woman She Is Today

    From the little girl she once was to the incredible person she is becoming, every chapter has added something beautiful. She carries her childhood memories, the lessons she has learned, and the dreams she continues to chase.

  8. 28 October · Chapter 08

    Another Beautiful Year

    Today celebrates every version of her: the little girl, the growing dreamer, the friend, the daughter, and the person she continues to become.
